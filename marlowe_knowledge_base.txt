ABOUT MARLOWE & FINCH
==========================================================

Marlowe & Finch is a small outdoor gear company based in Boulder, Colorado.
We design backcountry equipment for weekend backpackers and thru-hikers, with
a focus on lightweight three-season gear that holds up to real use. Our
products are sold through our website and a handful of independent outdoor
retailers. We do not currently sell through Amazon, REI, or other large
marketplaces.

We were founded in 2017 by two former trail crew leads who got tired of gear
that looked great in the store and fell apart on the trail. Every product in
our lineup is field-tested by our own staff before it ships.

Trailhead 2 Tent
-----------------------------------------------------------

The Trailhead 2 is our flagship two-person, three-season backpacking tent.
It is designed for moderate weather conditions from late spring through:early fall in temperate climates.

Specifications:
- Capacity: 2 people
- Trail weight: 4 lbs 3 oz (1.90 kg)
- Packed size: 18 x 5.5 inches
- Floor area: 29 square feet
- Peak height: 42 inches
- Doors: 2 (one on each side)
- Vestibules: 2 (8 square feet each)
- Fly material: 20D ripstop nylon with silicone/PU coating
- Floor material: 30D ripstop nylon with PU coating
- Waterproof rating: 2,000 mm hydrostatic head (fly and floor)
- Poles: Two color-coded DAC Featherlite aluminum poles
- Setup time: Approximately 5 minutes once you have practiced once or twice

What the Trailhead 2 is good for: Three-season backpacking in conditions
ranging from light rain to moderate wind. The 2,000 mm waterproof rating
handles steady rain well. The two-door design means you do not have to
crawl over your tent partner to get out at night.

What the Trailhead 2 is not designed for: Sustained heavy rain, alpine
storms, snow loading, or winter use. If you are heading above treeline in
shoulder season or expecting serious weather, look at our Summit 4 four-
season tent instead.

Available colors: Sage green, slate gray.
Price: $399.

Skyline 30 Sleeping Bag
-----------------------------------------------------------

The Skyline 30 is a synthetic-fill mummy sleeping bag rated to 30 degrees
Fahrenheit. We picked synthetic over down because synthetic fill insulates
even when wet, which matters more than weight savings for most three-season
backpacking in the US.

Specifications:
- Temperature rating: 30 degrees Fahrenheit (-1 degrees Celsius) comfort
- Fill: 4.5 oz Climashield Apex synthetic insulation
- Shell: 20D ripstop polyester with DWR coating
- Lining: 30D taffeta polyester
- Total weight: 2 lbs 8 oz (1.13 kg)
- Packed size: 8 x 14 inches in included compression sack
- Length options: Regular (fits up to 6'0") and Long (fits up to 6'6")
- Zipper: Two-way YKK zipper, right-hand side
- Draft tube: Yes, full-length along zipper
- Hood: Adjustable with single drawcord

Care notes: Front-loading washing machine, cold water, synthetic-fabric
detergent (we recommend Nikwax Tech Wash). Tumble dry on low with two clean
tennis balls to restore loft. Do not dry clean. Do not store compressed for
long periods; we ship a large cotton storage sack for home storage.

Price: $189 (Regular), $209 (Long).

WARRANTY POLICY
==========================================================

All Marlowe & Finch gear is covered by our Trail-Tested Warranty. The
Trail-Tested Warranty covers manufacturing defects and workmanship issues
for the lifetime of the original owner.

What is covered:
- Seam failures
- Zipper failures (excluding damage from forcing a stuck zipper)
- Buckle and strap hardware failures
- Pole breakage during normal use
- Delamination of waterproof coatings within the first three years of
  ownership
- Down or synthetic fill loss through stitching

What is not covered:
- Normal wear and tear (abrasion, fading, fabric pilling)
- Damage from animals, including rodent damage to fabric or food storage
- Damage from improper care (machine washing a tent, storing a sleeping bag
  compressed long-term, leaving gear in direct sunlight for extended periods)
- Damage from use outside the product's intended conditions (using the
  Trailhead 2 in winter alpine conditions, exceeding the load rating on the
  Summit 65, etc.)
- Loss or theft

How to file a warranty claimY
1. Email warranty@marloweandfinch.com with your order number, photos of the
   issue, and a brief description of how the issue occurred.
2. Our warranty team reviews claims within 5 business days.
3. If approved, we will either repair the product, replace it, or issue
   store credit, at our discretion.

The warranty is non-transferable. We do not honor warranty claims on gear
purchased secondhand.

-----------------------------------------------------------
Returns
-----------------------------------------------------------

You can return any unused item within 60 days of the purchase date for a
full refund to your original payment method.

To qualify as "unused," items must be:
- In original packaging with all tags attached
- Free of dirt, pet hair, scent, and any signs of outdoor use
- Free of modifications (no aftermarket patches, sewn-in identifiers, etc.)

Items that have been used outdoors are not eligible for return, even if they
appear clean. We have seen too many returned tents with pine needles in the
seams. If used gear has a manufacturing defect, it falls under the Trail-
Tested Warranty above, not the return policy.

To start a return:
1. Email returns@marloweandfinch.com with your order number.
2. We will email you a prepaid return label within one business day.
3. Pack the item in its original packaging and drop it off at any UPS
  location.
4. Refunds are issued within 5 business days of the item arriving back at
  our warehouse.

Sale items, gift cards, and customized gear (any product with a custom
embroidered name) are final sale and not eligible for return.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
===========================================================

Q: Is the Trailhead 2 fully waterproof?
A: The Trailhead 2 has a 2,000 mm hydrostatic head rating on both the fly
and the floor, with fully taped seams. It performs well in steady rain and
moderate wind. It is not rated for sustained heavy rain, alpine storms, or
snow loading. For those conditions, look at the Summit 4 four-season tent.

Q: How does the Skyline 30 compare to a down sleeping bag at the same
temperature rating?
A: A 30-degree down bag will typically be lighter (often by half a pound or
more) and pack smaller. The Skyline 30 trades some weight savings for
performance when wet. Synthetic insulation continues to insulate when damp,
where down loses most of its insulating value if it gets wet. We picked
synthetic for the Skyline 30 because most of our customers backpack in
conditions where wet gear is a real possibility.

Q: Will the Summit 65 fit me?
A: The Summit 65 comes in three sizes (Small, Medium, Large) matched to
torso length, not your overall height. Measure from the bony bump at the
base of your neck (C7 vertebra) down to the top of your hip bones (iliac
crest) along the curve of your spine. If you are between sizes, size down
for a tighter fit while hiking and up for more comfort while standing
around camp. If your pack does not fit when it arrives, email us within
30 days for a free size exchange.

Q: What's your sustainability policy?
A: We are a small company and try to be honest about what we do and do not do
on sustainability. Our shell fabrics are bluesign-approved, which means the
mills that make them meet standards on chemical use, water, and worker
safety. Our shipping boxes are made from recycled cardboard, and we do not
include printed catalogs or promotional inserts. We run a repair program
for out-of-warranty gear at cost rather than encouraging replacement. We do
not currently use recycled fabrics across the full lineup, and we are not
carbon neutral. We would rather tell you what we actually do thane make
claims we cannot back up.


Q: How should I clean a tent that has been used in the field?
A: Set the tent up in a shaded area. Wipe down the fly and floor with a
damp sponge and mild soap. Rinse with clean water. Let the tent air-dry
completely before packing it away; storing a damp tent is the number one
cause of mildew on the fly. Never put a tent in a washing machine; this
voids the warranty.

Q: Can I order replacement parts (poles, stuff sacks, etc.)?
A: Yes. Email parts@marloweandfinch.com with your product name and the part
you need. Most replacement parts ship within a week. There is no charge for
warranty-covered replacements; out-of-warranty parts are sold at cost.

Q: Are your products vegan?
A: Our synthetic insulation, fabrics, and hardware are all vegan. None of
our current products use down, leather, or animal-derived adhesives. If
this matters to you, you can buy any product in our lineup with confidence.


Q: Do you have physical stores?
A: We do not run our own retail stores. A small number of independent
outdoor retailers carry select product from our lineup. Email
hello@marloweandfinch.com for a current list of retail partners near you.
